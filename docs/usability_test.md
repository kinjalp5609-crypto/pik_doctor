# Usability test sheet (5 first-time users, no help given)

Run it in 10 minutes per person. Use a real phone in airplane mode, one leaf (or the sample photos), and say only:
"Please find out what is wrong with this leaf." Do not explain buttons. Stay quiet and note what happens.
Ask them to use the language they speak at home. Record with their permission, or just take notes.

After the task, ask three questions and write the answer in their own words:
1. "What did the app tell you to do?" (checks they understood the steps)
2. "Would you trust it? What would you do before spraying?" (checks the safety message landed)
3. "What was confusing?"

Summarise as one line for the one-pager, using only your real numbers:
"X of 5 first-time users reached a diagnosis without help in a median of Y seconds; we changed Z afterwards."
